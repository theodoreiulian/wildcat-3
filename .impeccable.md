## Design Context

### Users
The project owner runs WildCat-3 locally on a Mac and wants to connect Kraken and operate the bot without editing configuration or remembering terminal commands.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, restrained. Explain actions in familiar language and distinguish simulated money from real orders at every decision point.

### Aesthetic Direction
Chosen for this implementation: a restrained, portfolio-first professional trading terminal. A true black canvas, compact SF Mono typography, cool grey structure, green live/positive data and red risk/negative data make it technical without becoming dense. A compact top-bar switcher moves the left workspace between Portfolio and a dedicated Activity view; Protection owns the bottom of the Portfolio screen, while the tall read-only process log remains fixed in the right rail. Connections stay behind a top-bar settings control. Square controls, no external fonts or assets. The panel is its own Electron window: the top bar doubles as the title bar, clearing the macOS window buttons and carrying a full-screen control beside the settings button, so the trading terminal can take the whole display without browser chrome around it.

### Design Principles
- Keep account setup and daily operation in one place.
- Show saved, checking, running and failed states explicitly.
- Never present a successful connection as proof of profitability or guaranteed protection.
- Keep secrets out of response payloads and avoid technical detail in ordinary workflows.
- Support keyboard use and narrow screens.
- Colour carries meaning, never decoration: grey structures; green/red report selection, sign and severity.
